PDC Assignments

Welcome to PDC Assignments, a curated collection of computer programming assignments related to parallel and/or distributed computing (PDC). PDC instructors may pick and choose assignments that best fit their course objectives. PDC Assignments are designed for experiential learning, aka learning by doing.

Outcomes

Students who successfully complete these assignments will gain hands-on experience applying PDC concepts and abstractions within the context of a PDC technology, such as multithreading, message-passing, etc.
